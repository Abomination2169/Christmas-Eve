# Christmas Eve v1.0.0 — audio credits and redistribution status

The public source repository currently contains all four Ogg Vorbis files, as does private v1.0.0 Build #131. These are technically extractable, including from a QMOD. The credits below identify their stated provenance; **not all underlying redistribution rights have been independently verified**, and the presence of these files in the repository should not be read as a statement of clearance.

## Menu music

1. **We Wish You a Merry Christmas** — recording by **HD-Studio**, supplied by the project owner from Pixabay; traditional Christmas carol. Packaged as `we_wish_you_a_merry_christmas.ogg`. The owner reports a favorable Pixabay response about use in projects, but the actual written reply and track-specific permission for extractable QMOD distribution have not been reviewed here. Exact item URL remains unverified.

2. **Dance of the Sugar Plum Fairy** — recording/arrangement by **Kevin MacLeod** (incompetech.com); original composition by **Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky** (*The Nutcracker*). Packaged as `dance_of_the_sugar_plum_fairy.ogg`. Source: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/index.html?isrc=USUAN1100625 . Licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Audio was transcoded to Ogg Vorbis for the mod. Credit and license link must accompany redistribution; final recording identity/source match should be verified before publishing.

3. **Christmas Lights — Post-2002 Musical Christmas Lights (2024 Re-Recording)** — the project owner states they made **their own 2024 recording of their own musical Christmas lights**, not audio extracted from YouTube. Packaged as `M668_Christmas_Lights_5m22_click_removed.ogg`. The supplied audio was converted to Ogg Vorbis and edited to approximately 5:22, removing the opening click.

   **Inspiration / special thanks:** *90's Christmas light music with slide show*, by **Christmas everyday**:
   - Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwiBpHOmwL4
   - Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@christmaseveryday9462

   The inspiration credit is not a claim of permission from the video creator, and it is not a claim that their recording was sampled. Ownership of the user's new recording is distinguished from any copyright that might subsist in underlying programmed musical arrangements. The arrangement provenance/permission still needs verification.
